# ERT Hub — Product Requirements

## 1. Purpose

A centralized workspace for managing EJUST Racing Team operations:
tasks, projects, events, weekly reports, and submissions.

## 2. Problem

Work is tracked across scattered chats, sheets, and meetings. Operations
cannot easily see who is doing what, what is overdue, what is blocked,
and which reports are missing.

## 3. Users

- Team Member: sees own tasks, updates progress, submits weekly reports
- Department Head: manages department tasks, reviews submissions
- Operations: monitors all departments, projects, reports, blockers
- Leadership: views team-level progress
- Admin: manages users and permissions

## 4. Core Features

- Task management (list, board, My Tasks)
- Projects and milestones
- Dashboard
- Calendar
- Weekly reports
- Submissions (deliverables)
- Events
- Announcements
- Team directory

## 5. Functional Requirements

### Tasks

- Create, edit, delete tasks
- Assign a member and collaborators
- Set deadline and priority
- Update status and progress
- Add comments and checklists
- Keep an activity history

### Weekly Reports

- Members submit a weekly report linked to their tasks
- Report blockers and request help
- Operations can see who has not submitted

(We will fill in the other features as we reach them.)

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

- Works well on phone and laptop
- Secure login and role-based access
- Fast and simple to use
- Easy to maintain as the team grows

## 7. MVP (proposed, to confirm)

- Login
- Tasks (create, assign, status, progress, deadlines)
- Basic dashboard
- Weekly reports

## 8. Future Features

- Calendar, events, announcements
- Google Drive integration
- Push and email notifications
- Weighted project progress

## 9. Success Criteria

- Operations can see overdue and blocked work in under 10 seconds
- Most members submit weekly reports through the app
